Department ATC/Tender conditions are treated as final when they conflict with GeM conditions. Both sources remain stored separately.

📎 Source Document References

TenderMate stores the source document metadata here. The actual PDFs are not uploaded to GitHub by this browser-only version.

GeM source: Record the GeM condition separately. If Department ATC provides a conflicting condition, the Department condition is final for TenderMate's final-applicable field.
Priority Rule: If a Department ATC/Tender condition conflicts with a GeM condition, TenderMate uses the Department condition as the applicable condition.
FINAL APPLICABLE CONDITIONS
Department ATC/Tender conditions take priority where a Department value is present. Where the Department field is blank, TenderMate retains the GeM value as the available source. Review the source PDFs before bid submission.

⚖️ Conflict Register

Record a specific GeM vs Department conflict. The Department condition is recorded as the final applicable condition.
