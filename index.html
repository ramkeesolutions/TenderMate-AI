📑 Tender Analysis & Tender Register

TenderMate stores GeM and Department ATC separately and determines the governing precedence from the tender documents. An explicit precedence clause in the tender is recorded; otherwise the TenderMate default is Department ATC priority.

📎 Source Documents & Automatic Analysis

Select the GeM PDF and Department ATC PDF. TenderMate reads text from the PDFs in your browser and fills the structured tender fields. The PDF files themselves are not uploaded to GitHub.

GeM source: Record the GeM condition separately. If Department ATC provides a conflicting condition, the Department condition is final for TenderMate's final-applicable field.
Priority Rule: If a Department ATC/Tender condition conflicts with a GeM condition, TenderMate uses the Department condition as the applicable condition.
Governing precedence: Not yet determined.

⚖️ Conflict Register

Record a specific GeM vs Department conflict. The Department condition is recorded as the final applicable condition.

The structured list is used for future shortfall checking. The raw list is preserved for anything that needs manual review.
